> OPEN: Delete is offered on every file, but L-13 makes a settled statement read-only.
> OPEN: "Delete" must sit behind a confirm dialog (A2 Danger). The button variant is not stated.
> OPEN: "with the rupee symbol intact" — does ₹ appear in the header only, or inside each amount cell? Amount cells containing ₹ are text, not numbers, in Excel.
> OPEN: Columns, date format and filename for each of the four exports are not specified. Product C-12 says "maintenance spend per unit" and "the current tenant list"; L-14 says "tenant list", with a date range.
> OPEN: Subject "FY 2025-26" is fixed text. Is it derived from the chosen date range or the current financial year (April–March)?
> OPEN: No upload control on L-14; uploads happen elsewhere (L-09, L-11 — also unspecified).
