## Interactions

| Trigger | Result |
|---|---|
| Modal opens | Draft is requested and a skeleton shows in the text area for up to 4 seconds. If it does not arrive, a plain template appears with a quiet note: "Wrote this from a template — edit as needed." The composer is never blocked by the assistant. |
| S01-BTN-WHATSAPP | Opens WhatsApp in a new tab with the current text pre-filled → the modal switches to a second state: "Did you send it?" with Yes and Not yet. Only "Yes" writes the reminder to the timeline. |
| S01-BTN-COPY | Copies to clipboard, toast confirms, and the same "Did you send it?" prompt appears |
| S01-SEG-TONE | If the message has been edited, warn before regenerating: "This will replace your edits." |
| S01-BTN-CANCEL | Closes with no record written. If edited, confirm first. |

**The "Did you send it?" step is not optional.** The app hands the message to WhatsApp; it cannot know whether the landlord actually pressed send there. Recording a reminder that was never sent would corrupt the escalation history and produce a wrong tone next time.

## Reminder ladder (from L-15 and product.md §10)

| Level | Tone | Default day | Rent lifecycle wording |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gentle | Day 3 | gentle nudge — drafted, landlord reviews and sends |
| 2 | Direct | Day 10 | direct reminder — states the amount and the date |
| 3 | Formal | Day 20 | formal notice — quotes the agreement clause |

Days are editable in L15-TBL-LADDER; the three levels are fixed.

> OPEN: Do logged calls (L05-BTN-LOGCALL, method "call") count in the escalation history?
> OPEN: Which messages use the assistant? > OPEN: S01-SEG-TONE for non-rent messages (vendor, renewal, maintenance update) — shown or hidden? The levels are rent-specific.
> OPEN: "Only 'Yes' writes the reminder to the timeline" — for non-rent messages, what is written, and to which timeline (L07-TML-HISTORY "message sent")?
> OPEN: "Not yet" — the modal's next state is not stated (close? return to editing?).
> OPEN: S01-BTN-EMAIL — does it also trigger "Did you send it?"
> OPEN: "Opens WhatsApp … with the current text pre-filled" — the recipient number is not stated (assumed the tenant's or vendor's phone, A2 Phone field +91).

> OPEN: OPENED FROM omits S-02 (S02-BTN-SENDLINK "Opens S-01"), L-06 (the notify-the-tenant toast) and L-11.
> OPEN: The "formal notice — quotes the agreement clause" — no agreement clause text is stored anywhere in the data model.
> OPEN: S01-HDR "Flat 4C" — unit numbers are unique only within a property. Does the header show the property?
> OPEN: Server-side vs client-side drafting and what data the assistant receives are not specified.
